# Sero – Elevator Pitch

## Core

Sero is an AI stack for e-commerce.  
It helps teams grow faster by turning data into decisions and automating execution across the entire customer journey.

---

## One-liner

Turn your e-commerce data into a decision engine powered by AI.

---

## What Sero Does

Sero connects your data, understands your customers, and activates that intelligence across your channels.

No heavy IT.  
No fragmented tooling.  
Just one AI-driven system that improves continuously.

---

## The Problem

Most e-commerce teams:

- Work with fragmented tools
- Spend time on manual processes
- Struggle to activate their data
- Miss opportunities across the customer journey

---

## The Solution

Sero replaces disconnected tools with one AI layer that:

- Collects and structures data
- Learns from behavior and patterns
- Activates insights in real-time

---

## Key Capabilities

- AI-driven customer support (Assist)
- Smart product discovery and guidance (Guide)
- Conversational experiences (Chat)
- Visual product experiences (Visualize)
- Automated content and SEO (Create / SEO Autopilot)

---

## How It Works

1. Capture data across all touchpoints
2. Enrich and structure it with AI
3. Activate it across channels, content, and customer interactions

Always learning.  
Always improving.

---

## Impact

- Higher conversion rates
- More value per customer
- Lower operational workload
- Faster decision-making

---

## Positioning

Sero is not another tool.  
It’s the AI layer that sits on top of your stack and makes everything smarter.

---

## Closing

From data platform to decision engine.  
From manual work to autonomous growth.

## Claude Prompt

Write in a clear, high-level, C-level tone:

- Focus on business impact
- Keep it concise and structured
- Avoid technical jargon unless necessary
- Emphasize outcomes over features
- Use short, scannable sentences

Position Sero as:
- an AI layer
- a decision engine
- a driver of scalable e-commerce growth
