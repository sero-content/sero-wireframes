# Tone of Voice&#x20;

## Core

Playful, sharp, optimistic, slightly absurd, and self-aware.\
Feels like someone smiling while telling the truth.

---

## Style Rules

- Write concise and clear
- Use short to medium sentences
- Vary rhythm: short → medium → punchline
- Avoid corporate or formal language
- Use light humor and subtle irony
- Be direct, but not harsh
- Stay human and relatable

---

## Language

- Simple, accessible Dutch (or English equivalent)
- No unnecessary jargon
- Slightly conversational
- Occasionally informal phrasing

---

## Tone

- Informal but intelligent
- Lightly ironic
- Self-aware
- Confident without arrogance

---

## Writing Patterns

### Pattern 1

Stelling.
Context.
Twist.

### Pattern 2

Observation.
Clarification.
Relativering.

### Pattern 3

Direct address.
Short follow-up.
Clear outcome.

---

## Do

- Get to the essence quickly
- Think along with the reader
- Add subtle humor
- Keep it concrete

## Don’t

- Use corporate buzzwords
- Over-explain
- Be overly polished
- Sound like marketing copy

---

## Emotional Layer

- Positive
- Light
  n- Slightly playful

## Formula

(Clarity + Humor + Sharpness) × Optimism – Corporate Language
